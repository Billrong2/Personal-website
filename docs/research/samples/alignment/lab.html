Spearman rank correlation ρ

Both axes run from 1 (easiest) to 6 (hardest). A diagonal is perfect rank agreement.

Inspectable calculation

ρ = 1 − 6Σd² / [n(n² − 1)]

Here n = 6 with no tied ranks. This shortcut requires distinct ranks.

The paper’s behavioral proxies are richer than these authored ranking profiles. This demo focuses on the comparison principle.
