# Asset and source notes

This homepage is an original implementation of the selected fourth design direction: a pixel cyberpunk city with purple/pink terminal rain, an animated rooftop character, and four personal-world entrances. The canvas scene and interface graphics are drawn in code. No Jack V. Le template code, photographic city asset, or Edgerunners material is included in this version.

The three world pages reuse this project's original design candidates: Life uses the warm pixel room from candidate 03, Research uses the green Matrix/CRT interface from candidate 02, and Play uses the purple/pink fusion city from candidate 04. The earlier candidate files remain separate.
